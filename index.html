The cost of fragmentation

Why unstructured family care eventually breaks down

The problem isn’t a lack of love. It’s that the right update rarely reaches the right person at the right time.

The missed-routine spiral

A delayed medicine or skipped meal becomes visible only after symptoms appear. Tiny gaps compound because nobody owns verification.

The 2:00 AM false alarm

A silent phone triggers international calls, panicked relatives and frustration—when your parent was simply asleep.

The informal-help black hole

Maids, cooks and guards know what happened on the ground, but their updates disappear inside fragmented voice notes and calls.

Medication routines

A reminder is useful. Knowing what happened next brings peace of mind.

CareCircle supports family-configured medicine schedules through WhatsApp and voice calls, then helps capture a simple confirmation from the parent or an authorised caregiver.

CareCircle coordinates reminders and confirmations. It does not prescribe medication, change dosage or provide clinical advice.
